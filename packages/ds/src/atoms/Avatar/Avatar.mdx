# Avatar

Use `Avatar` to show a person: their picture, or their initials.

- **Level:** atom

<Canvas of={Stories.Initials} />

## When to use

- Use `Avatar` in lists of people, comments and the account menu.

## When not to use

- Do not use `Avatar` for a company logo or a product image.

## Rules

1. `name` is required. It is the accessible name and the source of the initials.
2. When the picture does not load, the initials show.
