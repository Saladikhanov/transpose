# Spinner

Use `Spinner` to show that something is loading.

- **Level:** atom

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## When to use

- Use a spinner for a wait of about 1 to 10 seconds.
- Change `label` to say what loads, for example "Saving…".

## When not to use

- Do not use a spinner for a whole page that loads. Show the layout with placeholders.
- Do not show more than one spinner on a screen.

## Rules

1. Screen readers announce `label` through `role="status"`.
2. With reduced motion, the spinner does not rotate.
